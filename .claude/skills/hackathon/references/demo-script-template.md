# Demo script template (~3 minutes)

Structure proven by `RECORDING-SCRIPT.md` (CORTEGE's WARDEN demo). Reuse the cadence and timing;
swap in the flow chosen per SKILL.md step 2.

## [0:00-0:15] Intro
One or two sentences: what the product is, who it's for. No feature list yet.

## [0:15-0:45] Problem + solution
- Lead with a stat or concrete scenario that makes the problem real.
- State what existing solutions miss, and the one-sentence reason this approach is different.

## [0:45-1:30] Live demo — the one chosen flow
- Click through the single flow picked in SKILL.md step 2, start to finish.
- Narrate what's happening technically as it happens (what's being called, what's being decided),
  not just what's on screen.

## [1:30-2:15] One deeper technical or product moment
- Pick the single most differentiated piece of engineering (e.g., an audit trail, a scoring
  heuristic, a learning/escalation mechanism) and show it in ~30-45 seconds. Not a feature tour —
  one thing, shown well.

## [2:15-2:45] Platform/architecture pitch
- Why this isn't a one-off hack: what's generic/reusable about the architecture, what numbers back
  up "this is real" (tests passing, cost optimization, latency, etc.).

## [2:45-3:00] Close
- One sentence on where this goes next and why it gets harder to displace over time (the moat).
- Thank you.

## Tips
- Don't rush; 3 minutes is plenty — pause between sections.
- Click slowly, let the UI render.
- If something breaks live, narrate what it would do rather than debugging on camera.
- Record at 1280x720 or 1920x1080, other apps closed.
- Publish the final recording to a public link (YouTube/Vimeo/Loom) before submitting — see
  `references/lessons-from-judges.md` on why this specifically matters.
